Three.js Conf Paris

Producer, April – September 2026

The first conference ever dedicated to Three.js, held in Paris in September 2026. Before this project I didn't know what Three.js was. Then I discovered the projects, the endless possibilities — and above all, the energy of its community.

My work was entirely backstage: project management, negotiation with every party involved, social media, goodies and venue decoration.

During the preparation I built my first tool — with Three.js itself, 100% vibe-coded.

A 3D event map generated from the venue's 2D CAD file. The team edits it together, like Figma: drop in design files, add items, measure sizes. It let everyone see what the venue would look like, and exactly what we needed to buy. I'm still improving it.
